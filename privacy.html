Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MyMaawa ("the app") is a free Islamic guidance app. We respect your privacy.

What we collect

No accounts, no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only thing the site counts is visits: a small counter on our own server adds 1 to the totals for the day, month, year and country. It saves no IP address and no personal data. To show "online now", your browser sends a random ID that is kept for 5 minutes only.

What stays on your device

Your language, theme, reading position, progress, favourite duas, quiz scores, Life Score answers and zakat calculator entries are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device and we cannot see them. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-party services

Fonts and all other files are served from this website. Only when you press play on a Quran recitation does your browser load the audio and its verse timings directly from the recitation libraries listed under Credits (mp3quran.net, everyayah.com, quran.com, archive.org); like any website, they can see your IP address for that request. Prayer times use your location only if you allow it, rounded to about 1 km, and it stays in your browser. The website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security.

Children

The app includes content for children and collects no personal information from anyone.
